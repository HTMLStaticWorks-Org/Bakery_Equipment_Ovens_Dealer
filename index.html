Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Selecting Commercial Bakery Equipment

Answers to help you plan your oven configuration, facility preparation, and quote request.

The ideal oven depends on your product mix and batch volume. Commercial Deck Ovens use conductive stone hearths and radiant heat, making them ideal for crusty artisan breads and sourdough. Convection Ovens circulate heated air across multiple trays for pastries, cookies, and cakes. Rotary Rack Ovens accept full roll-in pan racks for high-volume wholesale bread, rolls, and bun production. Our specialists often configure hybrid oven lines combining two types.

A Spiral Mixer rotates both the stainless-steel bowl and the spiral hook simultaneously, developing gluten gently with minimal friction heat—essential for yeast-raised bread, pizza, and bagel doughs. A Planetary Mixer uses interchangeable attachments (whisk, flat beater, dough hook) rotating around a stationary bowl, making it versatile for cake batters, meringues, buttercreams, fillings, and smaller dough batches.

Sharing your approximate daily production volume, target baked goods, available floor footprint, doorway delivery clearances, and available utility connections (single-phase vs. three-phase electrical, natural gas, water lines, and ventilation hood specs) helps our specialists recommend compatible equipment models right away.
