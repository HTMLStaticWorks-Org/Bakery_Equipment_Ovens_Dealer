Commercial Bakery Lifecycle Support

Complete Support Beyond Equipment.

From initial kitchen workflow consultation and equipment sizing to installation coordination, preventative maintenance, and replacement parts assistance, OVENCRAFT PRO supports your bakery at every operational stage.

Equipment Consultation

Production Workflow & Kitchen Footprint Assessment

Every commercial bakery has distinct production rhythms. An artisan sourdough bakery requires high-thermal-mass stone hearths and strong steam generation, while a high-output pastry commissary prioritizes reversible dough sheeters, programmable retarder-proofers, and multi-tray convection airflow.

  • Evaluation of target bake menu, batch sizes, and shift schedules
  • Assessment of ceiling heights, hood clearances, and loading path widths
  • Review of electrical phase availability, gas pressure, and steam water lines
Equipment Selection Assistance

Synchronized Sizing Across Mixing, Proofing & Baking

A common mistake in bakery outfitting is purchasing mismatched capacities—such as pairing a high-volume spiral mixer with an undersized proofer or oven deck layout. Our selection specialists calculate end-to-end throughput so dough flows seamlessly from bowl to bench to oven.

  • Pan-count and rack-capacity matching between proofers and ovens
  • Comparison of gas vs. electric deck and rotary rack oven architectures
  • Modular deck selection allowing future vertical expansion

Installation Coordination

Structured Site Readiness & Commissioning Coordination

Heavy commercial bakery ovens and industrial dough systems require careful delivery logistics and utility preparation. Our technical team coordinates closely with your general contractor, electrician, and mechanical installers before your machinery arrives.

Pre-Delivery Spec Sheets Detailed rough-in diagrams for power, water filtration, drains, and exhaust vents.
Rigging & Uncrating Planning Verification of knock-down modular assembly requirements for tight doorways.
Startup & Calibration Initial stone-hearth curing guidance, thermostat verification, and damper checks.
Operator Orientation Walkthrough of control programming, safety interlocks, and daily care routines.
Ongoing Operational Care

Keeping Your Bakery Production Line Operating at Peak Reliability

Comprehensive post-installation services designed to protect your machinery investment and minimize unplanned downtime.

Equipment Maintenance Guidance

Preventative maintenance extends the operational lifespan of commercial ovens and heavy dough machinery. We provide model-specific maintenance schedules covering:

  • Steam generator descaling and water filtration cartridge inspection
  • Oven door gasket sealing checks and hinge tension adjustment
  • Mixer drive belt tensioning, gearbox lubrication, and scraper alignment

Dedicated Technical Support

When a question arises regarding control panel diagnostics, thermal calibration, or mechanical operation, our technical support desk helps your team or local technician troubleshoot efficiently.

  • Diagnostic guidance for digital PID temperature and humidity controllers
  • Electrical schematic and technical manual reference assistance
  • Coordination with qualified commercial kitchen service technicians

Replacement Parts Assistance

High-use components eventually require replacement under daily commercial production. We assist clients in identifying and sourcing the exact replacement parts for their equipment:

  • High-temperature silicone oven door gaskets, halogen bulbs & viewing glass
  • Heating elements, thermocouples, relays, and steam solenoid valves
  • Sheeter synthetic conveyor belts, synthetic scrapers, and mixer attachments

After-Sales Customer Support

As your bakery grows, your equipment requirements evolve. Our after-sales account team maintains records of your installed machinery to assist with future upgrades and accessories.

  • Accessory sourcing for extra mixer bowls, attachments, and roll-in racks
  • Planning support when adding modular oven decks or expanding proofer bays
  • Dedicated account records for streamlined repeat inquiries
