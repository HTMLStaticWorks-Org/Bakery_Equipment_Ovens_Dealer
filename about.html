Our Company Story

Bridging Industrial Engineering With Real-World Bakery Workflows

OVENCRAFT PRO was established to address a critical challenge faced by professional bakers: general restaurant supply houses rarely understand the thermal physics, steam requirements, and dough hydration demands of dedicated baking production.

Choosing between a stone-hearth deck oven and a rotary rack oven—or sizing a spiral mixer to match fermentation and proofing schedules—requires domain-specific expertise. We built our dealership around a focused mission: curating commercial-grade baking machinery and pairing it with knowledgeable consultation.

Whether a client is launching an independent neighborhood Boulangerie or expanding a multi-shift wholesale bakery line, our team provides structured equipment selection guidance that aligns floor space, utility requirements, and output goals.

Our Equipment Expertise

Quality & Reliability

Engineered for Continuous Commercial Duty Cycles

Commercial baking environments subject machinery to high ambient temperatures, airborne flour dust, humidity, and heavy daily mechanical loads. Every piece of equipment in our catalogue is evaluated against strict durability benchmarks:

  • Heavy-Gauge Stainless Steel Construction: Hygienic, corrosion-resistant surfaces built for rigorous daily sanitation.
  • High-Mass Thermal Retention: Heavy insulation and precision chamber sealing for consistent deck and rack heat stability.
  • Operator Safety & Ergonomics: Interlocked bowl guards, emergency stop controls, and cool-touch thermal viewing glass.
  • Service-Accessible Architecture: Organized electrical panels and accessible drive components that simplify routine maintenance.
